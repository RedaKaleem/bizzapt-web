[ 01 / PROBLEMS WE SOLVE ]

Start with what's going wrong.

01

Leads and enquiries slip through.

Requests sit in inboxes and follow-ups depend on memory.

What we look at
Where enquiries arrive, who owns them, how long replies take.
What we might build
Website forms connected to a CRM, automatic routing and reminders, and a view of every open enquiry.
02

Nobody trusts the numbers.

Every report needs reconciling before anyone can use it.

What we look at
Which systems hold which data, and where they disagree.
What we might build
One source of truth and a dashboard the whole team reads from.
03

The same work is done by hand, every week.

Copying, chasing and updating that never ends.

What we look at
Which steps repeat, and which need human judgment.
What we might build
Automation for the repetitive steps, with people kept on the decisions.
04

You've outgrown your tools.

Spreadsheets and off-the-shelf software no longer fit how you work.

What we look at
How the business actually runs, including its exceptions.
What we might build
Custom software or a web or mobile app built around your process.
05

Your business looks smaller online than it is.

Your website and brand don't reflect the quality of your work.

What we look at
What customers need to understand before they buy or enquire.
What we might build
A clear position, brand identity and website or online store.
06

You're launching something new.

An idea that needs to become something people can use.

What we look at
Who it's for and what it must help them do.
What we might build
A first version that's credible, usable and built to grow.
07

You want to use AI, but don't know where.

Everyone says you should. Nobody says how.

What we look at
Which tasks are high-volume and low-risk.
What we might build
AI that sorts, drafts or summarises, with a person approving what matters.
