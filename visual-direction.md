# Bizzapt visual direction — controlled creativity

## Principle

Complex underneath. Simple on the surface. Keep the site's current information architecture; mature the visual language consistently across its five routes.

## Shared system

- Foundation: existing warm paper `#f4eee6`, mineral grey `#e7e5e1`, ink `#211d1b`, charcoal `#29272a`.
- Brand anchor: original plum `#60435b`; restrained mauve wash `#ded4d8`.
- Relationship accents: muted teal `#789596` and muted gold `#9c855c`. Original logo colors remain unchanged. Accents are lines, markers or selected states, not a different bright fill for every module.
- Corners: 2px for containers and controls. Circular geometry belongs to graph nodes and functional indicators.
- Rules: 1px neutral dividers; connected elements share a surface. Shadows are reserved for modal separation, not routine cards.
- Typography: serif at narrative scale, sans at component scale, mono at annotation scale. Body text keeps comfortable line-height. Mobile labels remain readable.
- Motion: 180–360ms controlled transitions; relationship paths can draw once in response to selection. No hovering drift, rotation, bounce, elastic movement or decorative loops. Reduced-motion users receive immediate states.
- Texture: faint grain and local construction grids, with no full-screen animated texture.
- Composition: generous headline space, a continuous hero grid, indexed challenge rows, a network, a linear process and genuine work imagery. Vary composition without changing the page hierarchy.
- Responsive: diagram relationships become vertical sequences; challenge disclosures stack without shrinking their type; touch and keyboard expose all essential information.
